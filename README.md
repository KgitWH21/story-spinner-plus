# Story Spinner+

**A creative prompt tool for writers, worldbuilders, and musicians who are tired of starting with a blank page.**

---

## What It Does

Story Spinner+ gives you a spinning wheel packed with randomized creative prompts. Choose a mode, hit **SPIN!**, and get an instant character concept, story arc, or musical vibe to spark your next project.

### Three Modes

**Characters**
Spin up a character — archetype, personality traits, relationships, and the philosophical tensions that make them feel real. Great for novelists, screenwriters, game masters, and anyone who needs a cast that isn't boring.

**Stories**
Spin a story — genre, plot arc, narrative perspective, and the human questions at the heart of it. Whether you're outlining a novel or unsticking a scene you've been staring at for three days, this is your nudge.

**Music**
Spin a song — style, emotional tone, chord progression, and atmosphere. Built for producers and songwriters who want a starting direction without the blank-project paralysis.

---

## How to Use It

1. **Pick a mode** — tap Characters, Stories, or Music at the top
2. **Hit SPIN!** — the wheel lands on a random prompt combination
3. **Copy or save** — grab the result and take it to your work

### The Shuffle Button
The small shuffle icon next to SPIN! reloads the wheel with a fresh set of randomly drawn elements — useful when you want to explore a different range of prompts before spinning.

### My Library
Once you create a free account, every spin you save lands in **My Library**. From there you can:
- Browse all your saved Characters, Stories, and Music spins
- Pick individual elements from different spins and drop them into a **Builder Panel** — mix a character trait from one spin with a plot arc from another
- Copy your whole build to the clipboard in one click
- Rename your project to keep things organized

---

## Creating an Account

Tap **Sign In** in the top-right corner. You can create a free account or log in with an existing one. Your saves persist across sessions — nothing disappears when you close the tab.
